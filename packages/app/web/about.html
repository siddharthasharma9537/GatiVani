News on the Go — Telugu news you can listen to.

GatiVāni turns Telugu news into audio, so you can stay informed while commuting, walking, cooking, or working. Instead of reading, you listen — the app gathers the day's news and speaks it to you in natural Telugu.

What GatiVāni does

  • Live news, read aloud — the latest Telugu headlines, story summaries, and explainers from major Telugu news sources, narrated on demand.
  • Radio bulletins and podcasts — All India Radio's Telugu news bulletins, Mann Ki Baat, and popular Telugu podcasts, streaming in one place.
  • Markets at a glance — gold, silver, and market updates in a live ticker.
  • Your newspaper, narrated — upload a Telugu newspaper edition and listen to it section by section, like a personal radio edition of the day's paper.
  • Cricket scores — live match updates alongside the news.

Account and privacy

GatiVāni uses Google sign-in to keep your uploads and listening history tied to your account. Narration runs on a Gemini API key that you provide, stored only on your device. The app shows no ads and sells no data — read the full privacy policy.
