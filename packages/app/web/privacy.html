Privacy Policy — GatiVāni

Last updated: 13 July 2026

GatiVāni ("the app") is a Telugu news listening app available at gativani.sohum.cloud. This policy describes what data the app handles and how.

Account information

Signing in uses Google OAuth through our authentication provider, Supabase. We receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google, and use them solely to identify your account. We never see your Google password.

Your Gemini API key

Audio narration is powered by Google's Gemini API using a key that you provide. Your key is stored only in your browser or device's local storage. It is transmitted with your narration requests so they can run on your key, but it is never written to our servers or databases.

Uploaded documents

If you upload a newspaper edition, its pages are processed with Google's Gemini API to extract text, and the extracted content and generated audio are stored in our Supabase storage so your editions stay available to you.

News content

Headlines, articles, podcasts, and market data are fetched from public sources (RSS feeds, All India Radio, and similar). These requests are not tied to your identity.

What we don't do

The app shows no advertising, uses no third-party analytics or tracking, and never sells or shares your personal data with anyone.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data and uploaded documents are kept until you ask for them to be removed. To delete your account and associated data, email pothulapatisiddhartha@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.
